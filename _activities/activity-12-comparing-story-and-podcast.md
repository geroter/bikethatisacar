## Possible pre-teaching ideas

- Review Venn Diagrams -purpose and how to create them.
- Do a class compare and contrast with “The Bike that is a Car” and “Rosie Revere”.
- Re-listen to the story with the intent of comparing and contrasting- students should have a scrap piece of paper to jot down ideas as they hear them.

## 2 circle Venn diagram website

- [Compare and contrast 2 characters Venn diagram](https://timvandevall.com/venn-diagram-template-for-kids/)

## Brief explanation of activity

Either as a class or individually, students should set goals as to how many ideas they will put into each section of their Venn diagrams. Students should work independently, using their notes from re-listening to the stories, and fill in a Venn diagram on how the written story and the podcast are similar and different.
