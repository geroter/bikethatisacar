## Possible pre-teaching ideas

- Review parts of grammar/punctuation/fluency you want the student to focus on during their editing.
- Co-create anchor charts to help students when editing.
- Review using a dictionary and thesaurus.
- If doing the editing online- review usage of Word/Google docs and how to share work.

## Brief explanation of activity

When Aliyah wanted to send a story to “Story Pirates”, her Mom just scribed down the words, using very little punctuation (no paragraphs). She wanted to capture Aliyah’s imagination as true as she could, so that Aliyah’s “voice” could be heard in the words.

Obviously this story, if given the time and desire to be edited, could be even better. Remember these are the words and ideas of a six year old. Students can both focus on aspects such as proofreading for grammar or punctuation, but they can also edit the story making it smoother, being more descriptive, and clearer. Using a thesaurus might be ideal when asking the students to find some better and more descriptive words.

For younger grades, teachers can pick specific sentences from Aliyah’s story and work with the class to correct mistakes. This can be done on a wipe board with coloured markers as a class, and then a second sentence for students to work on independently.
