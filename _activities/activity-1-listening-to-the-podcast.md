## Possible pre-teaching ideas

- Learning about podcasts -what they are/who makes them/intended audience/purpose
- Brainstorm podcasts students know (Wow in the World, Circle Round, Brains On, But Why)
- KWL about podcasts
- Why students like the podcasts they do (humour, science, history, stories
- Media can inform and entertain
- Pre-listening strategies/predictions- who/what might they hear? (voices, music, special effects). How will they know if they are listening effectively/actively?

## Brief explanation of activity

Students will listen to the ‘Story Pirates’ podcast made based off of Aliyah’s story, "The Bike that is a Car". Students can have pre-listening conversations where they make predictions about the story based on the title. After listening to the podcast students can share what they thought of the story- what they liked/disliked/found humorous/would change/questions they have. Reflect that Aliyah was 6 when she wrote this- idea that anyone can share their stories/ideas. Sharing can be done in groups or whole class discussion.

For older grade students can listen to the podcast through an assigned critical lens

- **Purple Lens**- what are the facts in the story - retell main points
- **Red Lens**- what feelings does the story evoke?
- **Blue Lens**- what connections can you make to the story?
- **Yellow Lens**-what was enjoyable about the story?
- **Green Lens**- questions you have about the story?
- **Orange Lens**-what is the main idea of the story?

Have students break into groups with each lens colour in a group and discuss the Podcast from their perspective/lens. Or all the same lens can meet and compare observations.
