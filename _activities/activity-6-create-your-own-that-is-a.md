## Possible pre-teaching ideas

- Listen to the podcast and read the story.
- Brainstorm other ideas of unique and interesting objects.
- Create a word bank for words students might need.

## Brief explanation of activity

Whole class discussion on linking items that relate (shape, purpose, material) -a napkin that is a carpet, a banana that is a hammock. Students will create their own “\_\_\_\_\_\_ that is a \_\_\_\_\_”and illustrate it. Students can write a few words that describe it. How it works. What it does. Students can use a co-created word bank/box or personal dictionary or anchor charts.
